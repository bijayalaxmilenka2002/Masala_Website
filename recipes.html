Heritage of Odisha

Temple-Style Authentic Odia Dalma

Prep: 15 Mins | Cook: 25 Mins | Servings: 4–5 | Pure Vegetarian

Secret Ingredient
Subhadarshini Dalma Masala (100g)
Order on WhatsApp

Ingredients

  • • 1 cup Toor Dal (or Moong Dal), washed
  • • 2 cups mixed diced vegetables: raw papaya, pumpkin, raw banana, brinjal, colocasia (saroo), and beans
  • • 1 tbsp Subhadarshini Turmeric Powder
  • • 1.5 tbsp Subhadarshini Dalma Masala
  • • 1 tsp Subhadarshini Panch Phoran (for tadka)
  • • 2 whole dried red chillies & 1 bay leaf
  • • 2 tbsp pure desi ghee or mustard oil
  • • Salt to taste & freshly grated coconut for garnish

Preparation Steps

  1. Pressure cook the dal and chopped vegetables with water, salt, and Subhadarshini Turmeric Powder for 2 whistles until soft.
  2. In a separate pan, heat pure ghee. Add Subhadarshini Panch Phoran, bay leaf, and whole red chillies until they crackle aromatically.
  3. Pour the sizzling tempering directly over the hot cooked dal and vegetables.
  4. Sprinkle 1.5 tablespoons of Subhadarshini Dalma Masala over the simmering stew and stir gently.
  5. Garnish with grated coconut and serve hot with steamed rice or puris!
Coastal Seafood

Odia Macha Besara (Mustard Fish Curry)

Prep: 15 Mins | Cook: 20 Mins | Servings: 4 | Non-Vegetarian

Secret Ingredient
Subhadarshini Fish Curry Masala (100g)
Order on WhatsApp

Ingredients

  • • 500g fresh Rohu or Bhakur fish cut pieces
  • • 2 tbsp yellow & black mustard seeds ground with 4 garlic cloves
  • • 1.5 tbsp Subhadarshini Fish Curry Masala
  • • 1 tsp Subhadarshini Kashmiri Chilli Powder
  • • 1 dried mango piece (ambula) or 1 sliced tomato
  • • 1 diced potato & brinjal (optional)
  • • 3 tbsp cold-pressed mustard oil
  • • Salt and turmeric to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Marinate the cleaned fish steaks with salt and turmeric. Shallow fry in mustard oil until lightly crisp and set aside.
  2. In the remaining oil, crackle a pinch of Panch Phoran. Add the diced potatoes and sauté.
  3. Mix the mustard paste with water, Subhadarshini Fish Curry Masala, and Kashmiri chilli powder. Pour into the pan through a strainer to keep the gravy smooth.
  4. Add the dried mango slice (ambula) or tomatoes and bring to a boil.
  5. Slide in the fried fish steaks and simmer for 6–8 minutes on low flame until flavors infuse. Serve with hot steamed rice!
Royal Feast

Velvety Restaurant-Style Shahi Paneer

Prep: 15 Mins | Cook: 20 Mins | Servings: 4 | Vegetarian

Secret Ingredient
Subhadarshini Shahi Paneer Masala (100g)
Order on WhatsApp

Ingredients

  • • 300g fresh soft malai paneer cubes
  • • 15 cashew nuts soaked and ground to fine satin paste
  • • 3 ripe tomatoes and 1 onion boiled and pureed
  • • 1.5 tbsp Subhadarshini Shahi Paneer Masala
  • • 1 tbsp Subhadarshini Kasuri Methi
  • • 2 tbsp butter and 2 tbsp fresh dairy cream
  • • 1/2 tsp ginger-garlic paste

Preparation Steps

  1. Melt butter in a skillet. Sauté ginger-garlic paste, then stir in the smooth tomato-onion puree. Cook until oil separates.
  2. Add Subhadarshini Shahi Paneer Masala and salt. Sauté on gentle flame for 2 minutes to awaken the sweet cardamom aroma.
  3. Stir in the cashew paste and a splash of warm milk to achieve a silky, golden sauce.
  4. Gently slide in fresh paneer cubes and simmer on low for 4 minutes.
  5. Crush Subhadarshini Kasuri Methi between your palms, sprinkle on top with a swirl of fresh cream, and serve with naan or jeera rice!
